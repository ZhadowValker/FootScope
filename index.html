FootScope

Posture and gait screening

Record front, side and back videos, add foot photos, and get a printable report of knee, ankle, foot and walking measurements.

Patient

Recording

Videos and measurements stay on this phone. Nothing is uploaded.

FootScope is a screening aid based on 2-D camera pose estimation. It does not diagnose any condition. Results depend on camera position, lighting and clothing, and should be confirmed by clinical examination.

    How to take the photo

      Faded points are suggestions (heel / toe tip only). Check each one.

      Reference object for real-size measurements

      Lay the object flat on the floor beside the foot, in the same plane, and photograph from straight above or straight on. Tilt or a raised object makes the scale wrong.

      Tap the photo to place each point. Keep your finger down to see the magnifier and slide to the exact spot. Drag any point to adjust it.
