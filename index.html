FOOTSCOPE

Posture & gait screening

Record three short videos — front, side and back — and get knee, ankle, foot, posture and walking measurements.

Patient

Height is used to convert measurements into centimetres. Without it, only angles are reported.

Recording options

Videos and measurements stay on this phone. Nothing is uploaded.

FootScope is a screening aid based on 2-D camera pose estimation. It does not diagnose any condition. Results depend on camera position, lighting and clothing, and should be confirmed by clinical examination.

Set-up

    On the camera screen a level line shows whether the phone is straight — aim for green. The ∠ button shows live joint angles.

    What the recording does

    Gallery video: it should start with about 5 seconds standing still, then the walk, with the whole body in view.
